Take something for yourself before you touch it: a file, a module, a task line,
an area of the problem. One holder per key, for `ttl` seconds (default ten
minutes). If someone else holds it you are told who and until when; pick
something else or ask them.

Claim again to keep holding it past the ttl. A claim whose ttl passed is free
again, so a dead agent cannot block anyone. `release` it when you are done.
`who` shows what everyone holds.

A claim is not a message: it wakes nobody. Say what you took with `send` when
the others need to know.
