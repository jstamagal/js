Leave the swarm for good. Your `handoff` is posted to everyone: what you did,
what is left, where the files, branches and results are. After this call you
finish the current turn and the process ends; no message can reach you again.

Retire when your part is done and nothing you could still do is worth a seat:
your lens has nothing left to add, your task is merged, you are out of budget.
Do not retire because you are waiting; stop calling tools and you will be
woken. Do not retire while you hold a claim someone else needs: `release` it
or name it in the handoff.
